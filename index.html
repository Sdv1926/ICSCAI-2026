About The Symposium

Advancing Frontiers of Intelligent Computing

ICSCAI 2026 aims to bring together leading academic scientists, researchers, and industry practitioners to exchange and share their experiences and research results on all aspects of Smart Computing and Artificial Intelligence. In collaboration with our Technical Partner, AIT Thailand, the conference will provide a premier interdisciplinary platform to present and discuss the most recent innovations, trends, practical challenges encountered, and solutions adopted in the fields of CSE and AIML.

Roorkee Institute of Technology & Technical Collaboration

Roorkee Institute of Technology (RIT Roorkee), accredited with prestigious NAAC A++ grade, stands among the top 4% of engineering institutions across India. Located in the academic heart of Uttarakhand, RIT is committed to nurturing advanced research in computer science, robotics, and applied artificial intelligence. In collaborative alliance with Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Bangkok, Thailand, ICSCAI 2026 bridges the global academic community with high-rigor peer evaluations, international keynote addresses, and indexed open-access archival.
